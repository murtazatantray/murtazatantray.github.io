01 — Profile

About

I am a full-time Ph.D. Research Scholar in the Department of Mathematics, School of Advanced Sciences at Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT), Vellore, working under the supervision of Dr. Padigepati Naveen.

My doctoral research develops Physics-Informed Neural Networks for simulating the boundary layer flow of viscoelastic fluids. I focus on non-Newtonian fluid mechanics — second-grade and Maxwell fluids, nanofluid aggregation, radiation and slip effects — and on deep-learning frameworks that solve the governing nonlinear differential equations without relying solely on classical numerical schemes.

02 — Focus

Research Interests

🧠

Physics-Informed Neural Networks

Embedding governing PDEs into neural network training to solve forward and inverse problems in fluid mechanics.

🌊

Boundary Layer Flow

Non-similar and similar boundary layer flows of viscoelastic and non-Newtonian fluids over stretching surfaces.

💧

Computational Fluid Dynamics

Modelling heat and mass transfer, nanoparticle aggregation, radiation, and slip in complex fluid systems.

⚙️

Scientific Machine Learning

Adaptive loss balancing, deep-learning surrogates, and numerical methods for nonlinear differential equations.
